TOM / LOCAL AI

A hand, wherever you browse.

Start Tom, then paste the local connection link from its .state/session.json file. This development pairing lasts until you close the browser. Keep that link private; it grants access to your local assistant.

Right-click selected text to ask Tom, attach the visible tab as a screenshot, or search it. No page is read or captured until you choose an action. Edge and Chrome are the first targets. Brave is unqualified. Tor needs a separate integration.
